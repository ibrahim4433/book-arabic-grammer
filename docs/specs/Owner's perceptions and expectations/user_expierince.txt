# User Experience (UX) Expectations

The user experience must be professional, featuring tools that are intuitive and easy to handle.

Operational details should be displayed in a manner that is:
*   **Easy to Understand:** Clear visualization of processes.
*   **Controllable:** allowing user intervention.
*   **Practical:** Focused on utility.
*   **Detailed (Debugging-oriented):** Providing deep insights.

The ultimate goal is to ensure that every stage's details are displayed fully and professionally, effectively functioning as a **comprehensive and clear control room**.
