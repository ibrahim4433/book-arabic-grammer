# AI Image Generation Prompt for A4 Background

**Aspect Ratio:** 1:1.414 (A4 size vertical/portrait)
**Resolution:** High definition (minimum 2480x3508 pixels for 300 DPI print)
**Color Palette:**
- Background: White or very light off-white (#F0F4F5)
- Primary Accents: Muted teal (#458793)
- Secondary Accents: Sand/Camel (#C19A6B)
- Subtle shadows/lines: Dark teal (#2E4F55)

**Subject & Composition:**
Create a clean, professional, and elegant A4 page background template suitable for a printed Arabic grammar textbook. The main body of the page must remain plain white and entirely free of any patterns or distractions to allow text to be easily readable. 

**Decorative Elements:**
- Add subtle, minimalist geometric Arabic or Islamic motifs strictly confined to the page margins (top header area and bottom footer area). 
- Use the muted teal and sand/camel colors for these decorative borders or corner ornaments.
- The design should feel academic, timeless, and not overly crowded.
- Include a very faint, elegant watermark-style mandala or geometric star in the exact center of the page, but its opacity must be extremely low (less than 5%) so it does not interfere with the text.

**Style:**
Minimalist, academic, vector-art style, clean lines, flat colors with very subtle gradients, print-ready, professional layout.
