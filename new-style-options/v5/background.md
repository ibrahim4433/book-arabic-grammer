# Background Image Generation Prompt for Theme V5

**Image Specifications:**
- **Dimensions / Aspect Ratio:** A4 size (Vertical/Portrait, approximately 2480 x 3508 pixels).
- **Background Color:** Pure white or very subtle light off-white (#F2F3F4) to ensure maximum readability of the dense Arabic grammar text.
- **Style:** Clean, professional, minimal academic aesthetic, subtle geometric or traditional Islamic geometric patterns.

**Prompt for AI Image Generator (Midjourney / DALL-E / Stable Diffusion):**
> "A highly professional, minimalist A4 page background for an academic Arabic grammar textbook. The core background should be pure white to ensure maximum text readability. Around the extreme edges and corners, incorporate very subtle, elegant Islamic geometric patterns or minimalist arabesque motifs. Use a sophisticated color palette featuring muted grey-purple (#6C7A89) and deep slate blue (#34495E) for the line art and structural elements, with tiny, delicate accents of warm peach/amber (#F5B041). The center of the page must remain completely blank and unobstructed. Rendered in a clean, modern vector-art style, high resolution, soft aesthetic, 8k, print-ready."

**Design Philosophy & Notes:**
- The background must not distract from the heavy, dense text content.
- The grey-purple and slate blue provide a calming, scholarly feel.
- The peach/amber accents should be used very sparingly (e.g., thin border lines or small corner nodes) to draw attention without overwhelming the eye.
